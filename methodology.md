# Methodology — news-image-20261124, Round 3

## What this entry is

A one-page newspaper poster of **Tuesday 24 November 2026** — a single
1024×1024 PNG (`entry.png`), produced by `main.py`, that reports the day's
news as a broadsheet front page.

Layout: masthead ("TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026" + "40 STORIES · 7 REGIONS ·
EVERY FIGURE DATED + SOURCED") + 3 hero stories + 24 grid stories + 13
"Also on" stories + a 12-pin world map + a DATE CHECK box + a sources
footer. **40 distinct stories in total** (3 + 24 + 13 = 40).

Round 3 adds five systems on top of the round-2 poster:

- **Certainty badges** on all 13 illustrated stories (heroes 1–3 and grid
  4–13) — CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED / CLAIM — with a legend on the
  poster.
- **Drawn vector scenes** for every illustrated story: the 3 hero arts
  redrawn as real scenes (Sanofi: pill/pharma + $41B; 699 drones: sky,
  drones and interception; Hormuz: tanker + sea mine) plus 10 new small
  scene thumbnails for grid stories 4–13 (chip/ban, talks table, missile,
  Shanghai skyline/purge, chip chart, Pentagon/Canada, Nikkei chart,
  Doha/handshake, courthouse, satellite). All drawn fresh; no leader's
  image assets or text were copied — only the design idea (a drawn scene
  per numbered story). Grid stories 14–27 stay text-only.
- **Source tags** on every grid story (14–27 included) and per-line sources
  in the Also-on list.
- **"THE DAY IN UTC" timeline strip** — 8 timed events, times only from
  the verified list in this document.
- **"THE DAY IN NUMBERS" strip** — $41B · +8.99% · 699 · 58,645 · M5.3 ·
  $175 · 5,006 · 3 tankers (claimed). Every figure already appears on the
  poster.
- **Region tabs** summing to 40 (see the region split below).

## The event-date rule

A story belongs on this poster only if its event **happened on, or was
first reported on, Tuesday 24 November 2026 in some timezone**, verified
against the source article's own body/dateline or a primary wire — never
against an aggregator listing date. Aggregator dates never count.

Monday-night / Tuesday-morning overnight items are kept only when they
were first reported on Tuesday, and are labelled honestly as
**reported-Tue** (the event itself was Monday evening, US time, or
Monday night): Megaspeed (BIS filing Mon 23 Nov US, reported Tue 03:25
UTC), Zelensky's speech (Mon 23 Nov, wire Tue 00:30 UTC), and the
Poland drone incursion (Mon 23 Nov 21:49 UTC, reported Tue). The hero
detail for the Poland incursion says "Mon night, reported Tue" — the
incursion is never dated as a Tuesday event.

Stories whose true event date was not Tue 24 Nov were dropped and are
listed with their correct dates in the DATE CHECK box (see below).
